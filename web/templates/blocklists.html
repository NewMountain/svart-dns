Supported formats:
  • ||example.com^ - Block domain and all subdomains
  • example.com - Block exact domain
  • 192.168.1.1 - Block IP address
  • ! Comment - Comment lines (ignored)

Blocklists are fetched and parsed automatically. Lines starting with ! or # are treated as comments.
